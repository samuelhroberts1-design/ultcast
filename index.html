What is an Alt-Cast

The World of
Alternative Broadcasts.

An alt-cast is a second audio feed running alongside the official coverage of a live game — a completely different voice, angle, and energy on the same match. Maybe it's a biased angle in your team's favour, laid-back ex-player anecdotes, funny, analytical, or for kids — alt-casts can provide something for every fan.

The ManningCast, the NFL on Nickelodeon, F1 Kids, the Beckham & Friends cast. These are alt-casts. And they've proven, at the highest level, that audiences want alternatives when given the option.
