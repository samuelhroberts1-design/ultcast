8. Sport, copyright and data

Nobody owns a sporting event or its facts. Scores, results, player names and what happens on the field are public information, and you're free to watch a game and describe it in your own words. What is protected is other people's expression and material: the official broadcast itself (its pictures, sound and commentary), logos and trademarks, and data feeds supplied under licence.

That's why UltCast is built around your own voice. Call the game play by play, react to it, analyse it or make it funny, as long as the words are yours and you don't use material that belongs to someone else. The Creator Guidelines explain this in plain terms.

Welcome, Creator

UltCast exists because fans deserve more than one voice on every game. You're here to bring yours, whether that's calling every kick, breaking down tactics, or yelling at the umpire with your mates. These guidelines keep you, your listeners and UltCast out of trouble so we can keep doing this.

1. The golden rule: make it yours

Every cast on UltCast should sound like you. Your words, your take, your energy. That's the whole point of the platform, and it's also what keeps it on the right side of the line. Nobody owns the game, but the official broadcast belongs to the broadcaster, so bring your own voice and leave theirs out.

Any style is welcome: play-by-play calls, passionate one-eyed supporter casts, tactical breakdowns, comedy, laid-back chats, casts in other languages, or casts for kids. If it's your own commentary, it belongs here.

2. What you can do

  • Watch the game on TV or a stream while you cast. That's how most casts work;
  • Call the game as it happens, in your own words;
  • React, analyse, predict, joke and argue. Be biased if you like;
  • Mention public facts you can see for yourself, like the score, who scored, cards, and team and player names;
  • Cast from home, the car, the pub, or anywhere outside the ground;
  • Cast in any language.

3. What you can't do

  • No broadcast audio. Never stream the official commentary, TV or radio sound, studio segments or replays. Keep the TV out of your mic: use headphones, or turn the TV sound down or off. If broadcast audio is audible on your cast, we may end it.
  • No casting from the ground. Ticket and venue conditions at most grounds ban broadcasting or transmitting commentary from inside the venue. If you're at the game, enjoy it and cast another time.
  • No licensed data feeds. Don't read out live data from paid or official data services, such as advanced stats or live odds. Basic facts you've seen yourself are fine.
  • Nothing that looks official. Don't use league or club logos, and don't call your cast "official" or suggest a league, club or broadcaster is behind it. Use team names to say which game you're on, nothing more.
  • No defamation. Opinions are fine. False statements of fact about players, officials or anyone else are not, including rumours about injuries or private lives.
  • No gambling promotion. No betting ads or sponsors, odds, promo codes, affiliate links, paid tipster services, or real-money fantasy promotions. Casual predictions with your listeners are fine.
  • Nothing harmful or illegal. No hate speech, harassment, threats, sexually explicit content or anything unlawful.

4. Sponsorships

UltCast doesn't run ads. You may mention your own sponsors on your casts, as long as:

  • you clearly tell your listeners it's a sponsorship;
  • it isn't a betting or gambling brand;
  • it doesn't suggest a connection with a league, club or official broadcaster; and
  • it doesn't use a league's protected sponsor branding.

We may restrict other categories if we need to.

5. Tips and income

Tips from Listeners are processed by Stripe. UltCast keeps a 20% platform fee and you receive 80%, less payment processing fees. You're responsible for declaring tip income for tax. UltCast doesn't give tax advice.

Beta note: automatic payouts to Creators are still being built. Email support@ultcast.live for details.

6. Complaints and enforcement

We review reports from listeners and rights-holders and act on breaches. Depending on how serious it is, that can mean a warning, removing content, ending a live cast, suspending your ability to go live, or closing your account. If we act on a complaint about your cast, we'll tell you and you can give your side.

If you receive a legal notice or takedown request about your cast, let us know straight away at support@ultcast.live.

7. Your responsibility

You're legally responsible for what you say on UltCast, and you agree to cover UltCast for any claim that arises from your casts. We provide the platform; you own your voice.

In short: make it yours. Keep the TV out of your mic. Don't cast from the ground. Don't pretend to be official. Don't defame anyone. Keep it bet-free.
